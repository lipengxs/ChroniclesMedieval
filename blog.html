Record a platform claim before repeating it

Platform, pre-order, and cross-play questions should be tracked separately. Check the live Steam listing and the official Steam news feed; record the source date, platform, and exact claim. If neither source confirms a detail, “unconfirmed” is more useful than a guessed release window. Use the platform checklist before a purchase decision.
