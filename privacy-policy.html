This website

This policy describes an independent static fan website. It does not describe the game or Raw Power Games. This site has no game login, payment form, cloud saves or in-game support system.

Cloudflare provides website delivery and may process request information such as IP address, browser details and requested URLs. Some pages use Google Analytics to measure page visits and clicks on official-source links. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on browser settings and service configuration.

Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for their processing details. You can manage cookies and site storage in your browser.

Advertising and external content

The site no longer includes its previous advertising integrations. Historical images are served from this site. Links to museums, Steam and the official developer website take you to independently operated services with their own policies.

Game-related data

This website does not collect game progress, game account credentials, game payments or gameplay history. For questions about information held by the game or a storefront, use that service’s official privacy and support channels.
